Composer / Pianist

Juliet Gjone

Juliet Gjone is a Norwegian Australian film composer and pianist based in Tasmania. She has played piano since the age of seven, and graduated from Music Industry College in Brisbane, QLD. She is currently studying Commercial Music Creation at the Conservatorium of Music in Hobart. Her current work includes composing the score for an Estonian feature film. Alongside her composing practice, Juliet has taught piano privately since 2022 shaping every lesson around the individual, their goals, and what excites them about music.
